## 01 | Profile

I am a **Project Assistant Professor at Kobe University, Japan**, working on robotic manipulation, Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, and embodied AI.

My research combines a strong foundation in **robot dynamics, nonlinear and robust control, adaptive control, and flexible manipulators** with current work on **language-conditioned manipulation, multimodal perception, policy inference, and real-world robot execution**.

My broader goal is to develop robotic systems that can operate reliably in complex and uncertain environments by combining **model-based control, robot learning, perception, and autonomous decision-making**.

## 03 | Featured Robotics Project

### [π0.5 × xArm7 — Real-Robot VLA Handwash Bottle Picking](https://github.com/KLochan/pi0.5-xArm7-handwash-pick)

Real-robot Vision-Language-Action deployment using **π0.5 / OpenPI** on a **UFactory xArm7** with a **UF G2 gripper**, dual **Intel RealSense D435i** cameras, **ROS 2 Jazzy**, and **LeRobot**.

- ~100 real-robot demonstrations
- dual-camera visual observations
- 15-step action horizon
- verified real-robot pick–lift–release execution
- typical policy inference around 100 ms on an RTX 5090 workstation
- end-to-end pipeline from demonstration collection to real-robot execution

> More robotics projects will be added as they are released.
