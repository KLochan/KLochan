## about

I am a **Project Assistant Professor at Kobe University, Japan**, working in the **CINAPS Lab**. My research focuses on **robot dynamics and control, Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, autonomous robotic manipulation, perception, and embodied AI**.

My research trajectory began with modelling and robust control of flexible robotic manipulators and expanded toward nonlinear and adaptive control, underwater manipulation, visual servoing, agricultural robotics, and learning-based autonomous manipulation.

At Kobe University, my current work investigates how **vision, language, perception, policy inference, and robot control** can be integrated to enable robotic manipulators to perform autonomous tasks from natural-language instructions.

---

## research interests

My research interests include:

**Vision-Language-Action Models · Autonomous Robotic Manipulation · Robot Dynamics & Control · Nonlinear & Robust Control · Embodied AI · Visual Servoing · Flexible Manipulators · Agricultural Robotics**

My long-term research vision is to develop **robust, adaptive, and intelligent robotic systems** that can operate reliably in complex and uncertain real-world environments by combining **model-based control, multimodal perception, robot learning, and language-conditioned autonomy**.

## selected project

### [π0.5 × xArm7 — Real-Robot VLA Manipulation](https://github.com/KLochan/pi0.5-xArm7-handwash-pick)

Real-robot Vision-Language-Action deployment using **π0.5 / OpenPI** on a **UFactory xArm7** with dual Intel RealSense cameras, ROS 2 Jazzy, and LeRobot.

- ~100 real-robot demonstrations
- language-conditioned manipulation
- verified pick–lift–release execution
- typical policy inference around **100 ms** on an RTX 5090 workstation

[Project repository →](https://github.com/KLochan/pi0.5-xArm7-handwash-pick)
